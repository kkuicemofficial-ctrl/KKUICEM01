Academic rounds

A look back at the knowledge and skills at the heart of the competition.

01 · Academic

Written examination

Every KKUICEM begins at the desks — timed written rounds testing the breadth of emergency medicine knowledge before teams move to the floor.

02 · Academic

Case presentation

Teams take the stage to walk judges through real clinical reasoning, defending their diagnosis and management plan in front of a live audience.

03 · Academic

Procedural stations

Hands-on skill stations put teams through the practical side of emergency care, from airway management to trauma response, under the eyes of examiners.

04 · Academic

Toxicology quiz bowl

A fast-paced, game-show-style round testing toxicology knowledge on the buzzer, with points on the line and the crowd watching every answer.

05 · Academic

High-acuity simulation finals

The final round: full-scale resuscitation scenarios that push finalist teams through the highest-acuity cases of the competition.

06 · Academic

Judging & deliberation

Behind every round, a panel of judges scores performance, deliberates closely, and decides who advances.
