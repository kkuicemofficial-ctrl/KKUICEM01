Opening ceremony

Before the competition begins, teams get to know each other through games and casual conversation, breaking the ice ahead of five packed days.

Welcome dinner

A shared meal on the first night, setting a warm tone for the days of competition ahead.

Delegates get a look beyond the competition halls — a tour of Khon Kaen University's campus, hospitals, and the city itself.

Cultural night

An evening dedicated to Isan culture — from Muay Thai to traditional crafts and music — giving international teams a taste of the region.

06 · Non-Academic

Awards ceremony

The competition closes with medals, trophies, and recognition for the teams who rose to the top.

Farewell & fun fair

One last night together: a fun-fair send-off with games, music, and goodbyes before teams head home.
